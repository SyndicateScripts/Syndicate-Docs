## Getting your files

Purchases are delivered through **[FiveM Keymaster](https://keymaster.fivem.net/)**,
under the Cfx.re account you gave at checkout. Updates arrive the same way —
re-download from Keymaster.

We cannot send resources through Discord.

## Support

Support runs through tickets on our Discord, so your server details and
purchase history stay private and in one place.

**Have ready:** product name and version, your framework, the complete console
error, and steps to reproduce.

Support covers installation, configuration and defects. It does not cover
conflicts with third-party resources, custom feature development, or modified
core resources.
